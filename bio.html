Bio

Zach Marano is an aerospace engineering student at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, and an analyst at Type One Ventures, a deep tech venture firm, where he leads aerospace and defense deal flow. He also leads corporate development at Lunar Outpost. At UTK, he rebuilt the Student Space Technology Association from three members to more than 300, with student teams building rockets, hypersonic vehicles, lunar robots, and a lunar CubeSat. He cares about building a real economy in space, especially on and around the Moon, and writes about space markets, policy, and philosophy.
